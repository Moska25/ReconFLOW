Matching is re-runnable. It rebuilds every automated match from scratch and produces the same result for the same data. Manual confirmations are preserved and their rows are excluded from re-matching.

What the validator checks

Each row is validated on its own. A bad row is refused with a reason and the rest of the file still posts, because rejecting four hundred good rows over one malformed date is not a safety feature.
